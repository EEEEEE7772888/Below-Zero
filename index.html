              HOW TO PLAY

              • You start with worms. They get lots of bites, but only tiny fish go for them.
              • Bites are random. When something bites, you'll see FISH ON! and it stays hooked until you reel it in. Reel in before a bite and your hook comes up empty.
              • Fish go into your inventory, which has no limit. Sell them for coins right away, or keep them and look at them any time.
              • Your biggest fish of each kind is on the world leaderboard while you keep it. Sell it and it comes off.
              • Spend coins in the Bait Shop. Better bait brings bigger fish that pay more, but you have to wait longer.
              • The size limit goes up until a fish bites. The clock keeps running even with this page closed.
              • Weedline opens after 1 hour of total fishing. The Deep Hole opens after 8 hours.
              • In the Gear Shop, buy up to 4 holes. Each hole has its own lake, spot and bait, and a red flag pops up when one gets a bite. Tap the HOLE buttons to switch.
              • Big fish can snap a weak line and get away. Your starter line holds 15 lb. Buy stronger line in the Gear Shop.
              • Lake passes take you to Northern Lake and Glacier Lake, with new fish to find.
              • Each lake hides one legendary fish. Read the clues in the Fish Journal.
              • Any fish can rarely come up golden (worth 10 times as much) or albino (5 times).
              • Every month is a new season with its own leaderboard. The top 3 win coins and an exclusive Champion cap.
              • Check MENU every day for a daily challenge, and earn coins from achievements.
              • Spend leftover coins in the Clothes Shop to dress up your angler.
              • Bigger bait gets fewer bites, but a fish that bites late in a long wait can be huge.
              • Time of day matters. Panfish and pike like daytime. Walleye, catfish and flatheads come out at night. Dawn and dusk are prime time, when trophy fish are twice as likely. Check the NOW sign at the top.
              • Each cast's luck is decided when you cast, so refreshing won't get you a better fish.

              FISH ALERT (computer only): in Chrome or Edge it opens a tiny window that floats on top of your other apps and flashes when something bites. In other browsers it sends a silent pop-up notification instead. It only works on the game's own website, not inside Claude.

              Press Space to cast or reel in on a keyboard.
